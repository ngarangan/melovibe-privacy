Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

MeloVibe ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the MeloVibe mobile application.

Information We Collect

MeloVibe does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the main features of the application.

Depending on the features you use, the application may process information such as search queries, playback information, playlists, liked items, and downloaded content locally on your device.

How We Use Information

Information processed by the application is used to provide features such as music search, playback, playlists, history, background playback, sleep timer, and downloads.

Third-Party Services

MeloVibe may use third-party services to provide certain application functionality, such as retrieving publicly available music and video content or providing advertising services. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Storage

Information such as playlists, favorites, and playback history may be stored locally on your device. We do not operate a user account system for MeloVibe.

Children's Privacy

MeloVibe is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or MeloVibe, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the application store listing.
